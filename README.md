# Before you change bank details

Free companion to the existing BaptistSec security resources. For small businesses checking supplier payment requests, not a new training topic or qualification.

## Use it

Open `payment-check.html` (`index.html` is an identical copy for the hosted site) in a browser, or use `payment-check-card-and-record.pdf`. The PDF has a one-page check card, a blank record and a fictional example with sources. `blank-payment-check-record.docx` is an editable record for your own protected files.

The checklist helps you pause a payment, compare it with your own records, call a trusted supplier contact and follow your own approval process. It cannot inspect an invoice, verify a person or bank account, approve a payment or guarantee that fraud is absent.

## Privacy

The downloaded HTML works offline. There are no text-entry fields, bank-detail collection, analytics, connections or saved answers. Choices stay only in the open page and clear on reload, closing or Reset answers. Print uses your browser and may save or print the current page and answers. Source links open external websites only when selected. A hosted copy's hosting provider may receive normal page requests; this checklist does not send your answers.

## Limits

General guidance, not legal or financial advice. A real sender account, a check of an email's technical information or a bank name match cannot prove a payment request is genuine. Follow your bank's current guidance and your business's payment controls. If money may already have gone to a fraudster, contact your bank immediately. Recovery is not guaranteed.
